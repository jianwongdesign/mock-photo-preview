About

Hi, I'm Chris.

A visual storyteller working across camera, canvas and comedy.

I tell stories with whatever the story needs: a camera, a pencil, or a punchline.

Some stories are best told in a single frame. Some need motion and sound, some need to be drawn, and some just need to make you laugh. Working across all four means each project gets the medium that suits it, not the only one I know.

Whether it's a brand campaign, a wedding, a custom illustration or a skit for the feed, the goal stays the same: honest work that people remember and want to share.

Away from the camera, my partner and I make couple comedy about the small, everyday chaos of sharing a life.
